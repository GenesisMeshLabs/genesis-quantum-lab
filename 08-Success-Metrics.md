## Risk Indicators

### Watch Metrics (Early Warning Signs)

| Metric | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Budget variance | >10% | Escalate to steering committee |
| Schedule delay | >2 weeks | Replan and communicate |
| Team turnover | >20% | Recruitment/retention review |
| Uptime SLA breach | <95% | Root cause analysis, remediation |
| Audit findings | CRITICAL | Immediate remediation plan |
| Detection rate drop | <70% | Detection engineering review |

### Contingency Actions

- **Budget overrun:** Negotiate cost reduction or expand timeline
- **Schedule delay:** Reduce scope or add resources
- **Key person loss:** Activate knowledge transfer protocols
- **Audit findings:** Establish remediation task force
